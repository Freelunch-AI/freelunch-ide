# Planning And Architecture

Use this capability for `plan` and when implementation uncovers a material design gap.

1. Read the issue, repository instructions, relevant specifications, current code, and recent related decisions.
2. Separate stated requirements from assumptions. Ask only questions whose answers can materially change scope, architecture, safety, or acceptance criteria.
3. Research externally when a fact is unstable, niche, or absent from the repository. Prefer primary sources and record links.
4. Check the proposed change against the repository roadmap, architecture, technology choices, and branching strategy.
5. Produce testable acceptance criteria and identify error, security, migration, compatibility, and operational cases.
6. Choose the smallest architecture that satisfies the criteria. Record important tradeoffs and rejected alternatives briefly.
7. Break the work into ordered, independently verifiable tasks. Include a risk-based test strategy and an allowed-dependency decision.

For a small, well-specified change, keep the plan short. Do not manufacture a PRD or architecture document when acceptance criteria and a few tasks are enough.
