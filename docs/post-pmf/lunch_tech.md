# Lunch Tech | Tech Learning Platform: learn tech with AI-assistance, the right way

AI-powered Internal learning platform powerred by lunch-stem (also our creation) — the best STEM knowlegde base in the world that is also colloborative and frequently updated.  **Importance level: important.**

- **Takeaway ingestion**: allows you to continuly append takeaways from new video in youtibe channels, new blog posts in blogs, etc. Kind of a takeaway RSS feed.
- **Tutorials**
    - Targeted tutorials: makes an interactive html tutorial for you to understand something specific (e.g., formula in a paper), including all the prerequisites you will need, with references to sources in lunch-stem and quizes along the way.
    - Topic-based tutorials: makes an interactive html tutorial explaining complex topics, with references to sources in lunch-stem and quizes along the way.
- **Material improvement.** Take papers and hard to understand blog posts and transform **them into an a**nimated/interactive article with pre-requisite knowledge included.
- **Material prioritization**. Helps you prioritize which material, of your "to-study" list, to study next, based on: things you read before, things you wrote, questions you have, quality of the material, etc. It organizes things to learn by topic and highlights specific parts of the materials you should focus on (parts that will teach you something new);
- **Interactive Content writing**. Helps you build your own animated/interactive book/article about about an area of knowledge (e.g., LLM engineering) you are exploring in depth, helping you convert raw text latex to interactive animations. It gives book suggestions about structure, type of content, correctness of content and ease of understanding. Suggestions can be text, links, images, animations and interactive widgets. Also, compares your book/article with similar books/article to check how they overlap in content.).
- **Topic-based or Goal-based quizzes and exams:** makes quizzes and exams for you to test your knowledge.
