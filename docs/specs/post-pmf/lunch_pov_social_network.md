# The POV Social Network

A TikTok-like social network built entirely around **egocentric (POV) video**.

The network provides a continuous window into the **day-to-day experiences of random people around the world**. Unlike traditional IRL livestreaming, which tends to focus on third-person view, famous creators, and flashy events, users can experience ordinary life from someone else's perspective.

Commuting through Tokyo, harvesting coffee in Colombia, repairing fishing boats in Greece, working a night shift in Seoul, riding a motorcycle through rural Vietnam, cooking dinner for a family in Morocco, or attending a local festival in Brazil. The fun is discovering lives, places, and experiences that are completely different from your own.

The platform can also be a place to simply **relax and watch someone else's life unfold**. A viewer might put on a random stream of someone working a shift, listen to the conversations happening around them, follow the gossip, and chat with other viewers about what they are seeing.

## Product

**POV-Only Content**
All content is captured from an egocentric perspective, creating the feeling of directly experiencing another person's life.

**World Feed**
A TikTok-style feed of POV experiences from random people around the world. The viewer can continuously discover completely unfamiliar people, places, professions, and situations.

**Following Feed**
A separate feed containing content from creators the viewer follows.

**TikTok-Style Recommendation Algorithm**
A highly optimized recommendation system continuously learns what each viewer enjoys and recommends both **content that is currently hot** and content that is likely to be personally interesting to them.

**Live + Recorded**
Creators can livestream their experiences or upload previously recorded POV videos. Live streams do not need to be truly real-time: a delay of around one minute gives the platform enough time to perform moderation, face blurring, audio translation, and other processing before content reaches viewers.

**Real-Time Interaction**
Viewers can comment, interact with creators, and send **Super Chats** during live streams. Creators can see the live chat directly on their phones while streaming and can choose whether to receive chat notifications. Creators who want to focus entirely on their experience can turn notifications off and ignore the chat; creators who enjoy interacting can keep notifications enabled and engage with viewers in real time.

**Audio On/Off**
Videos can play silently by default, with viewers able to turn audio on whenever they want to hear the surrounding conversations and environment.

**Automatic Translation**
Speech and relevant audio can be translated in real time, allowing viewers to follow experiences regardless of the creator's language.

**Automatic Clipping**
AI identifies interesting moments during long streams and automatically turns them into short clips that can be discovered, recommended, and shared through the feed.

**World Search**
A search interface lets users find specific experiences, places, professions, activities, or creators—for example, searching for *Tokyo*, *car repair*, *cooking*, or *someone hiking in Patagonia*.

**Creator Rankings**
Creators compete across metrics such as viewership, engagement, hours streamed, and other platform-defined rankings, creating status and incentives to keep producing content.

**Creator Monetization**
Creators earn through advertising, Super Chats, and other monetization mechanisms.

**Free Capture Hardware**
Creators receive the hardware required to produce POV content for free, with the cost recovered from their future earnings.

**Tactile Creator Gloves**
Top creators receive increasingly advanced tactile-sensing gloves as milestone rewards, similar to YouTube's Silver and Gold Creator Awards. The gloves are both creator status symbols and increasingly capable data-collection devices.

## Privacy & Safety

**Real-Time Moderation**
AI continuously monitors streams and removes prohibited or irrelevant content, including non-POV footage, bathrooms, nudity, and other restricted material.

**Default Face Blurring**
Faces of people incidentally captured in the environment are automatically blurred before the stream reaches viewers.

**Creator-Controlled Blurring**
The creator can choose to keep the default privacy protection or selectively disable face blurring for individual people who are aware they are being recorded and want to appear on camera. The creator can also choose to disable blurring more broadly when appropriate.

This creates two possible experiences: people who want privacy remain blurred by default, while people who intentionally want to appear on camera can become part of the creator's content.

When a creator deliberately disables these protections, the platform can require explicit confirmation and make clear that the creator is responsible for obtaining any necessary consent and complying with applicable laws and platform rules. The platform would still retain the ability to intervene, remove content, or restore protections when required.

**Privacy Requests**
Anyone who appears in a recording can request additional face blurring or complete blurring/removal of their appearance.

## The Data Platform

The social network's underlying asset is the data it generates.

Every stream can provide synchronized **egocentric video, audio, and eventually tactile information**. Instead of collecting expensive robot demonstrations, the platform captures humans performing real-world activities and interacting with other humans, animals, objects, and environments continuously at consumer scale.

AI systems process the raw streams in real time, while offline AI agents clean, segment, deduplicate, annotate, and structure the data into high-quality training trajectories.

This creates a continuously growing dataset for training **gigantic robotics foundation models and tactile-aware world models**.

## Business & Long-Term Strategy

The initial business is the social network itself: **creator monetization, advertising, and Super Chats**.

The strategic advantage is the embodied-data pipeline created by the network.

As the platform scales, millions of people could continuously generate diverse real-world interaction data simply by using the social network. That data can provide a major advantage in building increasingly capable **robotics foundation models—and eventually the robots themselves**.
