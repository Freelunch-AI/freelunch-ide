# Lunch Business | Do all non-coding activities in a single place

Features:

- Hiring
- Asset & Supply Chain Management
- Finances
- Legal & HR
- CRM (Customer Relations Management)
- Graphics Design
- Marketing, Sales & Competitor Analysis
- Notion-like Knowledge Management
- Slack-like Internal Communications
- N8N-like Internal Automations
- Hybrid Code & No-code BI (queries, statistics, plots, deep internal research)
- Physical Task Management (hire physical workers, manage phsyical tasks, evaluate phsyical workers)
- Integrations with Notion, Slack, & Google Drive
