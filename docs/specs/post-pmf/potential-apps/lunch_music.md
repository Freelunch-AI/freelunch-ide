# Lunch Music | Music Making App: anyone can make songs & sell them to artists.

Platform for anyone to make hit songs and get paid for it.

Features:

- Digital Audio Workstation (DAW)
- A free “Mini Spotify” within the platform
- Song Licencing to Singers, with instant pay
- Song Publishing to Spotify, Tiktok and Youtube
- Multi-User Editing
- Git-like branching workflow
- AI-powered Voice Switch
- Chat-based AI Agent (can reference songs in youtube)
- AI completions
- AI improvement suggestions
- AI music theory lessons based on gaps identified in your songs
- AI-powered MP4 Song → MIDI
- AI-powered Hum → MIDI
- AI-powered Song Copyright Detector
- Royalty-free Song Library
- AI-powered Song Evaluation
