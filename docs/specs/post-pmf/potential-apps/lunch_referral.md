# The Talent Graph

### The problem

The best people are rarely discoverable through resumes, LinkedIn, or traditional recruiting.

The strongest signal is often:

> **Who do exceptional people personally recommend?**

Today, referrals are isolated events. The underlying network of trust is lost.

### The idea

Build a **Talent Graph** from recommendations between professionals.

Instead of:

`Alice → Bob`

we build:

`Alice → Bob → Carol → David → ...`

The platform **traverses the graph** to discover the best peopl in a given field.

Great people can be:
- recommended by other great people
- have big traces of recommendation
- have a lot of recommendations

Think **PageRank for talent**.

### The flywheel

`Recommendations → Talent Graph → Better hires → Outcome data → Better recommender reputation → Better recommendations`

### Incentives

- **Professionals:** earn money for successful recommendations.
- **Candidates:** get discovered through trusted networks rather than applications.
- **Companies:** access high-signal talent they could never find through conventional recruiting.

### The key insight

The product isn't a referral marketplace.

It's a **reputation graph of who knows exceptional people**.

> **Find the best people by following the recommendations of the best people.**
