# Lunch Clothes | Custom Clothes App: pick the type of clothing, tell the AI the artwork you want to make, preview yourself (or human models) using it, give feedback if needed, approve the final result, buy with 1-click, make a micro-brand out of it, share & profit from the micro-brand.

Think of *Shein*, but you can design & buy custom clothes by just telling what you want in english.

Features:

- Monetization: we get a cut from every piece of clothing sold and brand creators also get a cut from every piece of clothing they sell.
- Copyright: we automatically detect copyright issues (logos, etc) and suggest modifications.
- Limited Drops**:** Allow creators to set "scarcity." Only 50 units of a specific micro-band can ever be printed. This drives urgency and "hype" culture.
- Material Intelligence**:** Let the AI suggest fabrics based on the design (e.g., "This design works best on heavy heavyweight cotton" or "This pattern is perfect for activewear polyester").
- Feasibility Layer: after AI generates clothing spec, we have a feasibility layer that actualy verifies if is pratically feasible by one of our partner factories, if not, AI receives the feedbakc and remakes it.
- Collaborative Drops: ****Let two micro-brands "collab" on a drop to pool their audiences.
- Batch aggregation to make this idea feasible:
    
    Instead of:
    
    > 1 user → 1 unit
    > 
    
    You do:
    
    > 100 users → similar specs → 1 batch
    > 
    
    Example:
    
    - 100 black hoodies with different prints
    - same fabric, same cut → only print varies
    
    👉 This works because:
    
    - you batch the expensive parts (cutting, sewing)
    - only vary the cheap part (printing)
    
    This is how companies like Shein operate at speed.
