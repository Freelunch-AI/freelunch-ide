# Lunch Language | Language Learning App: learn from your real-world mistakes

**Spoken Grammarly — AI Coach for Real Conversations**

**Problem**

Millions of people can read and write a second language but **freeze or make mistakes in real conversations**. Current tools (like apps and courses) only work in practice mode. Once you’re actually talking to someone, **there’s no feedback on grammar, vocabulary, or phrasing**—so mistakes repeat and improvement is slow.

**Solution**

A **wearable AI conversation coach** that listens while you speak a second language and sends **private feedback to your phone after the conversation**.

It detects:

- grammar mistakes
- incorrect verb tenses
- unnatural phrasing
- wrong vocabulary choices

and suggests **better ways to say the same sentence**.

Think **“Grammarly for spoken conversations.”**

**How it works**

1. Earbuds or a small wearable mic capture your speech.
2. Speech-to-text converts it in real time.
3. An AI model analyzes grammar, vocabulary, and fluency.
4. After the conversation, the app shows:
    - mistakes you made
    - corrected sentences
    - better phrasing options
    - vocabulary suggestions.

**Example feedback**

You said:

> “Yesterday I go to the store and buy bread.”
> 

App feedback:

✔ Better: *“Yesterday I went to the store and bought bread.”*

✔ Tip: past tense verbs

**Target users**

- International students
- Immigrants working in a second language
- Professionals preparing for jobs abroad
- Language learners stuck at the “conversation plateau”

**Market timing**

Three trends make this possible now:

- near-perfect speech recognition
- large language models that detect grammar errors
- ubiquitous wearable microphones (earbuds, glasses)

**Vision**

Become the **default real-life language coach**—helping people improve every time they speak, not just when they study.

**Tagline**

> *Practice by living your life. Improve after every conversation.*
>
