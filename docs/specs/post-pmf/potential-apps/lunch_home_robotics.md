# Robot-Native Housing

**We build apartments designed for robots + humans, not just humans.**

This will enable **reliable home robots faster than any company trying to make robots work in arbitrary homes.**

General-purpose home robots face two fundamental problems: **homes are extremely complex and every home is different.** Robots must navigate arbitrary layouts, perform complex manipulation, and interact with environments designed entirely around humans.

Instead of forcing robots to learn every possible home, **we simplify and standardize the environment itself.**

We build **robot-native apartments** with standardized layouts, fixtures, appliances, robot-accessible interfaces, charging/docking infrastructure, and building APIs. This dramatically reduces the complexity and variability robots need to handle.

Our goal is to deliver **ready-to-live-in apartments with robots already installed**, capable of reliably performing tasks such as bathroom cleaning, laundry, dish washing, house cleaning, pet cleaning, and trash removal.

We will partner with a **construction company** to build the first robot-native residential building. But first, we will start with a single apartment proof-of-concept.
