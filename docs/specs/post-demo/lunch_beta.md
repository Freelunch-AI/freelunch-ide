# Lunch Beta

Besides the core platform team of Freelunch that builds the platforms, there will be another core team who's job is to just **beta test new prod deployments of Freelunch Comapanies**, pretending to be their their target user.

This is very important in the age were PRs are singificantly written by AI and Review is getting also more automated by AI as well. Its is crucial that deployments dont go directly to end-users, but instead go through a canary deployment where beta testing happens.

Porblems detected go directly to the Issues Tab of the Companie's Monorepo with all required evidence and how to reproduce the problem.
